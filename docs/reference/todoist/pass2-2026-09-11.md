document is the later measurement** — each such case is called out explicitly below rather

## How it was captured

- **Todoist web**, `app.todoist.com`, **dark theme**, **Free plan**, captured 2026-09-11 with
  "today" = Fri 11 Sep 2026. Driven through ego-browser; every figure read with
  `getComputedStyle()` inside `page.evaluate()`.
- **Leaf-node rule.** Pass 1's selectors were re-derived and one was found unsafe:
  `[data-testid="due-date-control"]` is a *button* that inherits white, and it contains a
  1×1 visually-hidden `Date:` label. Reading colour off it yields `rgb(255,255,255)` for
  every state. **The painted node is an inner `<span>`.** Two runs disagreed until this was
  isolated; all colours below are leaf-node reads.
- **Dark Reader is installed in this browser profile** (`data-darkreader-proxy-injected="true"`,
  extension `eimadpbcbfnmbkopoojfekhnkhdbieeh`). It was verified **not** to be restyling this
  site: zero `style.darkreader` nodes, zero `[class*="darkreader"]` nodes, zero
  `[data-darkreader-inline-bgcolor]` nodes. Measurements are uncontaminated. Anyone re-running
  this capture should re-check that, not assume it.
- **Corroboration:** every value pass 1 also measured reproduced byte-for-byte here
  (body `rgb(38,38,38)`, row `rgb(31,31,31)`, divider `rgb(61,61,61)`, row height 59px,
  title 14px/21px, P4 ring `rgb(169,169,169)`). That agreement is why the *new* values below
  are trusted.

## 10. Light theme — still GAP, but now well-evidenced

THEME-01 remains **blocked**. This was attempted exhaustively and failed; recording what was
ruled out so it is not re-litigated blind:

- All 8 themes in Settings (Todoist, Dark, Moonstone, Tangerine free; Kale, Blueberry,
  Lavender, Raspberry Pro-locked) plus a 9th undocumented `theme_gold` — **every one renders a
  dark background.**
- "Sync theme" and "Auto Dark Mode" toggled off and persisted — still dark.
- `prefers-color-scheme: light` forced via CDP and verified true inside the page — still dark.
- Chromium's own force-dark disabled via `Emulation.setAutoDarkModeOverride({enabled:false})`
  — still dark.
- Dark Reader ruled out as the cause (§ "How it was captured").

One transient `rgb(252,250,248)` near-white frame was seen once during a settings teardown and
could not be reproduced across ~6 attempts. **Not recorded as data.**

**The OS hypothesis was tested, and disproven.** macOS was switched to Light appearance and
`prefers-color-scheme: light` was confirmed `true` inside the page. Todoist still rendered dark.
Both remaining candidate themes were then explicitly selected and **saved with the Update button**
(a click on the swatch alone does not persist) and hard-reloaded:

| Theme, under OS Light | Settled `html` class | Settled body background |
|---|---|---|
| Todoist | `theme_todoist todoist_loaded` | `rgb(40, 28, 17)` — dark warm brown |
| Moonstone | `theme_moonstone todoist_loaded` | `rgb(27, 29, 30)` — dark |

The transient near-white `rgb(252,250,248)` seen in earlier attempts is now explained: it is a
**loading splash painted before the theme settles**, reproducible on the click but never on the
settled state after a hard reload. It is not a palette.

**Every candidate cause is now eliminated:** the in-app toggles, `prefers-color-scheme`,
Chromium's own force-dark (`Emulation.setAutoDarkModeOverride`), the Dark Reader extension
(verified 0/0/0 on all three contamination signals), and — checked last — the browser's launch
flags, which carry no force-dark switch.

**Conclusion: no light palette is reachable in this account/build.** The Free plan's four themes
(Todoist, Dark, Moonstone, Tangerine) all render dark; Kale, Blueberry, Lavender and Raspberry are
Pro-locked and were not touched. `THEME-01` is therefore blocked on **plan tier and product
behaviour**, not on method — which is a materially stronger statement than pass 1's "never
observed", and should be recorded as such rather than left as an open capture task.

The remaining options are a decision, not a measurement: re-scope `THEME-01` to derive meologue's
light palette from Todoist's published brand colours, or accept that meologue's light theme is its
own design and stop tracking light-mode parity rows.
